EkAyu for iOS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

EkAyu keeps the health information you enter on your device. The developer does not operate an EkAyu account service, collect your health information, sell personal data, serve advertising, or track you across apps or websites.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how the EkAyu iOS app (bundle identifier com.codecat15.ekayu) handles information. EkAyu is developed and maintained by codecat15 (referred to as “the developer,” “we,” or “us”).

2. Information you add to EkAyu

You may choose to enter or attach information such as:

  • profile names, relationships, dates of birth, sex, blood group, and profile photos;
  • medical conditions, medications, allergies, and other health-related details; and
  • medical report titles, types, dates, providers, facilities, notes, images, and PDF files.

This information is processed locally to provide the app’s profile, organization, search, preview, and sharing features. It is not transmitted to the developer or an EkAyu server.

3. Data collection and tracking

EkAyu does not automatically collect personal data, health data, usage data, diagnostics, analytics, advertising identifiers, or precise location. EkAyu does not use cookies or tracking technologies, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell or rent personal information.

The app does not include advertising or analytics services. It uses FactoryKit as a local software dependency for app configuration; that dependency does not receive app data.

4. On-device storage and security

Profiles, health details, report metadata, photos, and report attachments are stored in the app’s private storage on your Apple device. EkAyu applies iOS file protection to its stored health information and excludes its health-data store from device backups. Access is also protected by your device passcode and, when you enable the optional app lock in EkAyu, Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or your device passcode, depending on your device.

No security method is guaranteed to prevent every possible loss or unauthorized access. You are responsible for maintaining control of your device and passcode.

5. Device access and permissions

EkAyu uses protected device features only when you choose an action that needs them:

  • Camera permission: to capture a profile photo or medical report attachment.
  • Photos and files: Apple’s system pickers give EkAyu access only to the images or documents you select; EkAyu does not request broad access to your library.
  • Device authentication: to authenticate you when the optional app lock is enabled. EkAyu uses the authentication method available on your device, such as Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or the device passcode.

You can manage system permissions in the iOS Settings app. Disabling a permission may make its related feature unavailable, but does not send data to the developer.

6. Sharing and disclosure

EkAyu shares information only when you deliberately use an Apple sharing interface and choose a destination. For example, you can select one or more report attachments and share them through Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or another installed app. The selected recipient or service then handles that information under its own terms and privacy policy. Review the destination carefully before sharing sensitive health information.

We may disclose information if you voluntarily send it to us, or if disclosure is required to comply with applicable law. Because the developer does not receive your in-app health information, we cannot access or disclose it from an EkAyu server.

7. Optional support communications

If you choose “Email Feedback,” EkAyu opens your mail app with app version, build number, device model, and operating-system version included in a draft. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email. If sent, we receive the sender address, message, and included diagnostic details and use them only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve EkAyu. Do not include medical records or other sensitive health information in support messages.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Information entered in EkAyu remains on your device until you delete it, delete the related profile or report, use Settings → Delete All App Data, or remove the app. If you delete EkAyu, all data stored by the app on that device is permanently deleted, including every profile and all reports and attachments associated with those profiles. You can edit profiles and reports in the app. Deleted data cannot be restored by the developer because we do not keep a server or backup copy.

Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to address the request, maintain support records, protect against abuse, or meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete a support communication using the contact method below. We may need enough information to identify the communication, and legal exceptions may apply.

9. Children’s privacy

EkAyu is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an online service. A parent or guardian may use the app to keep a child’s information locally on the parent’s or guardian’s device. Only enter another person’s information when you are authorized to do so.

10. Health and medical notice

EkAyu is an organizational tool, not a healthcare provider, emergency service, diagnostic tool, or substitute for professional medical advice. The app does not connect to HealthKit and does not use health information for advertising, marketing, data mining, or sale.

11. International use

EkAyu’s in-app data processing takes place on your device. If you send support email or share information with another service, that provider may process information in other countries under its own privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if EkAyu’s features, data practices, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top will be revised when changes are published. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its App Store listing when appropriate.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email the developer at ekayuapp@gmail.com. Do not include health information, report attachments, credentials, or other sensitive personal data in your message.
