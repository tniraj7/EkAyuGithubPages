Help with using EkAyu on iPhone and iPad

For support, bug reports, or feature requests, email ekayuapp@gmail.com or use Settings → Feedback/Request Feature in EkAyu. Do not include medical records or other sensitive health information in your message.

Frequently asked questions

1. Do I need to log in to use EkAyu?

No. EkAyu does not use accounts or require a login. Create a profile and begin adding your information. For additional protection, you can enable the optional app lock in Settings. It uses the authentication available on your device, such as Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or your device passcode.

2. Can I use EkAyu on an iPad?

Yes. EkAyu supports both iPhone and iPad. Data is stored separately on each device because EkAyu does not currently provide cross-device synchronization.

3. Can I share a medical report with someone?

Yes. Sharing from a report’s details screen supplies the PDF or image attachments along with the report title and a text summary containing its type, date and time, and any provider, facility, and notes you entered. Notes are included in full, even when you select only some attachments. The receiving app determines whether it includes this title and summary. Sharing from an individual attachment’s preview supplies only that file. The entire profile is not shared automatically. Review the files and report details before sharing with a person or service you trust. The recipient or selected service controls its copy.

4. How do I report a bug or request a feature?

In EkAyu, open Settings → Feedback/Request Feature. Your mail app will open a draft addressed to the developer. Describe the problem or feature you would like, but do not attach medical records or include sensitive health information.

5. How do I access my iPhone records on my iPad?

EkAyu does not currently support cross-device sync or full profile transfer. You can share individual report attachments using options such as AirDrop, but this does not recreate the profile or report record on the other device. Cross-device support may be considered in the future based on user demand, but there is no committed timeline.

6. Where are my medical records stored?

Profiles, reports, photos, and attachments are stored only in EkAyu’s private storage on the device where you created them. They are not synchronized to iCloud or uploaded to an EkAyu server.

7. What happens to my data if I delete EkAyu?

Deleting EkAyu permanently deletes all of its data from that device, including every profile and all reports and attachments associated with those profiles. The developer cannot recover the data because EkAyu does not keep a server or backup copy.

8. Can I use EkAyu for family members?

Yes. You can create separate profiles to organize information for family members or other people in your care. Only store another person’s health information when you have their permission or are otherwise authorized to manage it.

9. What files can I attach to a report?

EkAyu accepts PDF, JPEG, and PNG attachments. You can add up to 15 files to one report, and each file can be up to 25 MB.

10. Does EkAyu provide medical advice?

No. EkAyu is an organizational tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services. Verify medical and medication information with a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.

Still need help?

Email ekayuapp@gmail.com. Include the app version, device type, iOS version, and a description of the problem when possible. Do not include medical records, report attachments, credentials, or other sensitive personal data.
